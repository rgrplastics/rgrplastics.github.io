About R G R Plastics

R G R is a trusted brand in the plastic packaging industry, representing two specialized companies: R G R Plastics and R G R Plastics and Traders.

R G R Plastics Established in 1989 in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, our group has grown from a small family-run workshop into a modern MSME, equipped with advanced blow-moulding and injection-moulding technology. R G R Plastics focuses on manufacturing high-quality HDPE, LDPE, and PP containers and lids for pharmaceutical, food, chemical, laboratory, and industrial applications. We offer flexible production runs, color and shape customization, and both virgin and reprocessed material options.

R G R Plastics and Traders extends our reach, ensuring efficient distribution and customer service across Tamil Nadu and beyond. With an emphasis on quality control, on‑time delivery and competitive pricing, we serve businesses that need dependable packaging solutions at scale. Contact us to discuss custom tooling, sample orders, or bulk supply.

Led by G. Veeramanikandan, Under his direction we’ve combined traditional craftsmanship with process improvements and new technologies to broaden our product range and improve service levels. His vision is to scale the company responsibly while maintaining the high standards that define our brand. our companies are committed to quality, reliability, and continuous improvement. With strict quality control, full batch traceability, competitive pricing, and on-time delivery, R G R stands as a preferred partner for businesses seeking dependable plastic packaging solutions.
