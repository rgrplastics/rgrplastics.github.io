About R G R

R G R is a trusted brand in the plastic packaging industry, representing two specialized companies: R G R Plastics and R G R Plastics and Traders.

R G R Plastics, established in 1989 in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, our group has grown from a small family-run workshop into a modern MSME, equipped with advanced blow-moulding and injection-moulding technology.

R G R Plastics focuses on manufacturing high-quality HDPE, LDPE, and PP containers and lids for pharmaceutical, food, chemical, laboratory, and industrial applications. We offer flexible production runs, color and shape customization, and both virgin and recycled material options. Our primary focus is on B2C/local markets and unregistered persons (URP), providing accessible and affordable packaging solutions for individual entrepreneurs and small-scale users.

R G R Plastics and Traders extends our reach, ensuring efficient distribution and customer service across Tamil Nadu and beyond. With an emphasis on quality control, on-time delivery, and competitive pricing, we serve businesses that need dependable packaging solutions at scale. This division caters primarily to small-scale and large-scale business vendors, offering bulk supplies, custom tooling, and dedicated support for established enterprises. Contact us to discuss custom tooling, sample orders, or bulk supply.

Led by G. Veeramanikandan, under his direction we've combined traditional craftsmanship with process improvements and new technologies to broaden our product range and improve service levels. His vision is to scale our companies responsibly while maintaining the high standards that define our brand. Our companies are committed to quality, reliability, and continuous improvement. With strict quality control, full batch traceability, competitive pricing, and on-time delivery, R G R stands as a preferred partner for businesses seeking dependable plastic packaging solutions.

Our Technology & Manufacturing Excellence

At RGR Plastics, our mid-sized manufacturing unit in Kumbakonam leverages modern technology and skilled craftsmanship to deliver high-quality plastic containers. Located in this vibrant Tier-2 city, we benefit from a robust industrial ecosystem, cost-effective operations, and proximity to key markets across Tamil Nadu and South India.

Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art automated blow molding and injection molding machines, capable of producing containers ranging from 10 ml to 1000 ml. We specialize in HDPE, LDPE, and PP grades, offering both virgin and recycled materials to provide eco-friendly and cost-effective solutions. Our technology allows for precise customization in color, shape, and design to meet specific industry requirements.

Quality is paramount in our production process. We implement rigorous quality control at every stage, from raw material inspection to final packaging. Our dedicated QC team ensures full traceability for each batch, adhering to industry standards and regulations. This commitment to excellence enables us to offer rapid manufacturing with consistent high quality and competitive pricing.

As a growing MSME in Kumbakonam, we combine technological innovation with local expertise, making us a reliable partner for businesses seeking dependable plastic packaging solutions.
