Who we are

A trusted packaging partner since 1989.

Established in Kumbakonam, Tamilnadu, RGR Plastics has grown from a family-run workshop into a modern MSME equipped with advanced blow-moulding and injection-moulding technology. We serve customers across Tamilnadu and support growing businesses beyond the state with dependable packaging supply.

RGR Plastics serves local markets, individual entrepreneurs, local retailers, and small businesses with accessible packaging solutions.

RGR Plastics and Traders supports business vendors across Tamilnadu and beyond with bulk supplies, custom tooling, and dedicated customer service.
